Fonts bundled with Everhour Widget
==================================

All fonts in this folder are free, open-source fonts downloaded from
Google Fonts, licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1, which allows
bundling and redistribution with software:  https://openfontlicense.org

  Inter, Geist, Doto, Space Grotesk, Space Mono, Poppins, Manrope, Outfit,
  Plus Jakarta Sans, Sora, DM Sans, JetBrains Mono

Each font's page, with its authors and full licence text:
  https://fonts.google.com/specimen/<Font+Name>   (e.g. .../specimen/Space+Grotesk)

The fonts are loaded privately by the widget; nothing is installed on the PC.
Apple's SF Pro and Nothing's NDot cannot be redistributed, so Inter and Doto
are used as the closest open alternatives.
